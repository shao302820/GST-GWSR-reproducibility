# Current experiment protocol

The 1,024 stations are split with random_state=42 into 819 outer-training and 205 outer-evaluation stations. The outer training table retains 815 stations after 3-IQR response cleaning. Within inner CV, the fitting subset alone supplies cleaning boundaries, predictor standardization and standardized log-response transformation. Validation stations are retained, and predictions are inverse-transformed before evaluation.

Nine adaptive bandwidths, k=20 through 60 in increments of five, are compared using five shuffled KFold partitions (random_state=42) and GP seeds 42,123,456. Predictions are averaged per station on their original scale, then the 819 pooled OOF predictions determine RMSE. This selects k=55; exact ties choose smaller k.

With k fixed, alpha={0,.25,.5,.75,1} and tau={.5,.6,.7,.8} produce 20 completed combinations. Each threshold has a freshly timed independent-search comparator: 90 batches,30 complete three-seed fold ensembles,14,742 task histories. The four-stage totals are360/120/58,968. The same pooled prediction-mean RMSE selects alpha=.75,tau=.5. Alpha=.75,tau=.6 has exactly equal metrics; ascending alpha then tau resolves the tie.

Local fitting uses Haversine great-circle distances with radius6371.0088km and adaptive bisquare weights. The target task descriptor is its8D standardized predictor vector. Similarity is alpha*exp(-d/median_positive_task_distance)+(1-alpha)*exp(-feature_distance/median_positive_feature_distance). Each query fold supplies unlabelled coordinates/covariates for these scales. KMeans(n_init=20,random_state=42) partitions query tasks into24 chains, using the recorded projected-coordinate helper. Within a chain the highest-affinity completed parent is considered; eligibility is similarity>=tau. Alpha can affect ordering, parent choice and eligibility.

The GP population is1000 and the objective is full-local weighted squared error plus0.01*node_count. The final expression minimizes this objective across all evaluated candidates. Finite candidate structures, including full-precision constants, are deduplicated and ranked in a historical archive capped at1000. An eligible child inherits the first800 (cyclically repeated if necessary) plus200 fresh random candidates. Its inherited initialization is reevaluated under the child objective before five evolutionary rounds. Roots and ineligible tasks use20 generations. All positions still search. No adaptive stopping or extra semantic/task filter is applied.

Bisquare construction returns normalized weights; the recorded engine copies and divides by their sum once at search entry, matching bandwidth-selection search. This floating-point operation is preserved in the portable code. There is no per-generation weight normalization. Fresh candidates, inherited reevaluation, all generations and prediction protection use the same local array.

The locked outer run evaluates205 stations for each of three seeds. Each GST seed has181 transferred5-round tasks and24 independent20-generation tasks. Independent GWSR uses205 independent20-generation tasks. A nonfinite prediction or absolute standardized prediction>=5 uses the local weighted response mean; inverse log predictions are clipped to[-5,10]. Training exceptions stop execution.

Final ensemble metrics use per-station arithmetic means of the three original-scale predictions. External baselines retain the predictions and original fitting settings from their aligned benchmark; their software environment and spatial implementations are recorded separately. Local GWRF and GWR/MGWR baseline distances remain their original implementations, while current GWSR/GST use Haversine.

Completed-batch wall time includes all parent searches, initialization, archive maintenance and chain dispatch. Shared preprocessing, chain planning, preflight, warmup, scoring and disk writes are excluded. One timing pair per seed is descriptive. The interrupted final attempt has at least27s additional work outside completed-batch totals.
